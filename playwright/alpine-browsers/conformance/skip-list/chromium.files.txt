# encodes them with PW's OWN bundled ffmpeg, not the browser. That PW ffmpeg is
# a STATICALLY-linked glibc binary that execs fine on musl (verified: its

# video.spec 28 passed; screencast.spec 12 passed (retries=2 — the empty-video /
